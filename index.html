Instructions

Events carry absolute start/stop times within their bar. Melody presses append after the latest melody/rest event; Click any note on the staff to select it (alignment, delete); right-click it — rests included — for Enter Precise Start/Stop Timing. Chord+ stacks up to 5 tones on one event. ⏱ Adjust takes off / adds on time in 0.1 / 0.01 / 0.001 s steps — whole chords with Chord+ on, single tones with it off. 🗑 Delete removes any clicked note, chord tones included; ↔ Duration drags a note/chord/rest to resize it, snapping (yellow) when its stop edge exactly meets another event's start (dragging forward) or stop (dragging backward). ✋ Grab drags a note/chord through time (sideways) and transposes it (up/down); ✎ Insert places a note wherever you click, at the preset duration; 🔗 Chord maker merges selected notes into one chord that takes the length of its lowest tone. ⟲ Loop repeats a chosen span — click a start note then a stop note, pick continuous or a count; entries inside are tagged ⟲. 🎨 Color paints notes (basic palette) and supports pitch-range rules (e.g., all F2–F3 green); labels on light hues use black text, on dark hues white. ▦ Quantize retimes selected tones as a 2/3/4/5/6(triplet)/8/9(arp)/16-set of equal back-to-back durations, steppable by ±0.1/0.01/0.001 s before or after applying. ▣ Select builds a multi-note group: drag any selected note to move the whole group in time and pitch, set every duration at once, or 🔇 mute the selection so it stays visible but silent in playback (mute persists through save/export). Mode toggle: Atonal is the free-time system; Tonal adds key signature (♯/♭ spelling), time signature with a beat grid and bar-end marker, tempo, and an instrument label — still strictly sine tones; switching modes starts a new composition (save first — the prompt will remind you). Theme toggle: Classic (white/black) or Gray/Yellow. ◫ Stencil (Beat Stencil Overlay, ♩ = 1 beat, default 60 BPM): pick whole/half/quarter/8th/16th/32nd (+ dot or double dot) and every key or MIDI press enters exactly that value in sequence — press length is ignored; with Chord+ on, entries stack into chords (max 7) until Next ▸; staff clicks select notes to re-value ("Set to ♩") or reshape with rhythm overlays (swing, tresillo, habanera, galop, and more); Quantized β (40–140 BPM) instead keeps hold recording and snaps each duration to the nearest beat value within max(±20–25 %, 20 ms). 🎹 MIDI input (Chrome/Edge): enable it, play a connected keyboard (e.g., Roland A-49), and key-down/key-up record notes at literal held durations with true polyphonic overlap; Chord group merges keys struck within 35 ms into one chord. Notes outside C2–F7 are ignored. Durations are uncapped; end a bar whenever you choose with ⏭ End bar. Undo reverses recordings, chord tones, chord splits, deletions, and timing edits. Saves live in THIS browser on THIS device (localStorage) — they do not travel with the HTML file. To move compositions between devices: Export all (one bundle file) or Copy all (clipboard → email/note), then Import / Paste import on the other device. Exported .json files are the durable master copies.
